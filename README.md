# Pathways & Mindset: Zero-dose vulnerability analysis
Analysis of Pathways segmentation in the Mindset dataset to support the Gates Immunization team. 

## Timeline
- Contingent on data sharing agreement from Kano
- Approx timeline to complete work: 2 months


## Final Deliverables
- Presentation and supporting documents shared with Gates Immunization team
- Potential: manuscript to submit for publication in peer-reviewed journal (pending outcomes and funding)


## Proposed Analysis Plan:
### 1. Develop a new bespoke Typing Tool to type data to the new Northern Nigeria segmentation. 
- Option 1: Successful mapping of NN DHS TT to Mindest data. Move forward with using NN DHS segmentation. 
- Option 2: Mapping does not reach sensitivity threshold. Decide if we want to move forward with NN DHS segmentation anyway, or a modified approach that improves sensitivity (e.g., collapse into vulnerable vs not vulnerable categories). 
- Option 3: Mapping does not reach sensitivity thresholds. Move forward using the previous Northern Nigeria segmentation. 
### 2. Conduct a descriptive review of outcomes by segment
- Produce a Kano-specific Pathways profile report to support interpretation and comparison, highlighting vulnerabilities that may be associated with vaccine uptake (e.g., care-seeking, autonomy)
- Provide descriptive results from Mindset survey by segment and pull out indicators of interest 
  - How outcomes map to segments (update of work from Mindset report)
  - Vaccination coverage by segment: ZD, under vaccinated, full vaccination
  - How BeSD-focused indicators and key access constructs vary across vulnerability segments
    - BeSD profile by segment
### 3. Compare vulnerability across different survey types
- Comparing vulnerability across the different survey approaches (leveraged by Mindset); how well did each approach identify vulnerable populations
### 4. Assess vulnerability measures and Pathways segmentation as a predictor of zero-dose outcomes
- Questions of interest: 
  - Are vulnerability measures as predictive (or more predictive) of vaccination outcomes compared to more proximal indicators of intent, access, and service quality/experience?
  - Does the Pathways vulnerability segmentation identify who is less likely to vaccinate, beyond the more immediate barriers and beliefs captured by BeSD—and, if so, through what pathways might vulnerability translate into vaccination outcomes?
  - Would using Pathways segmentation to target interventions be sufficient to identify populations most vulnerable to ZD?
- Methods to address questions
  - Exploratory analysis on associations between barriers to vaccination, vulnerability and vaccine outcomes 
  - Logistic regression to assess how predictive Pathways segments are of ZD outcomes, BeSD indicators
  - Structured modelling approach to account for causal pathways, e.g. Bayesian network analyses
